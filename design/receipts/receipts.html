BizBill — 58 mm Thermal Receipt Previews

Pixel-faithful previews of the ESC/POS receipt layouts from PRD Section 3.6 (58 mm paper, 48 mm printable, Font A, 32 columns). Each white column is one 220 px "paper" at ≈4.25 px/mm. Sample custom order PRNT-0231 (Sharma Printers, Printing & Custom Jobs profile) is shared by receipts 1, 2 and 5. All amounts are computed in integer paise with half-to-even rounding and reproduce the Section 3 figures exactly. Style legend: bold = ESC E 1; tall line = GS ! 0x01 (double height); huge KOT token = GS ! 0x11 (double width + height); inverted = GS B 1. Byte-level command mapping: escpos-spec.md (same folder).

Arithmetic verification (all in integer paise, GST rounded half-to-even)

  • PRNT-0231 (tax-exclusive): subtotal 1190.00 − 5% discount 59.50 = taxable 1130.50; CGST = SGST = round-half-even(113050 × 0.09) = 10174 p = 101.74; pre-round total 1333.98; round off +0.02 → total_amount 1334.00; advance_paid 500.00 (UPI) → balance_due 834.00. Payment status: PARTIAL.
  • KIRN-0847 (tax-inclusive): shelf subtotal 683.00. Back-calculated taxable per slab: 5% → 233.33 (Atta), 0% → 45.00 (loose rice), 12% → 245.54 (butter), 18% → 100.00 (Colgate); taxable value 623.87; CGST 5.83 + 14.73 + 9.00 = 29.56 = SGST; pre-round 682.99; round off +0.01 → TOTAL 683.00. Paid in full via UPI → PAID.

Hardware note — QR placement on 58 mm

  • This page mirrors the Section 3.6 ASCII layout, which draws QR #1 and QR #2 side by side. On real 58 mm paper (48 mm printable) a 25 mm Tracking QR (FR-3.16) plus a ≤37 mm Payment QR (FR-3.19) cannot share a row; the 58 mm renderer prints them stacked, centered, each with its caption, and only 80 mm prints them side by side (FR-3.20). See escpos-spec.md §4.3.
  • QR boxes here are drawn to relative scale: QR #1 ≈25 mm (V4, 33 modules, EC M, 6 dots/module); QR #2 ≈30–35 mm (V5–V8 depending on VPA/shop-name length, EC M).
  • Receipts print amounts without thousands separators to fit the 32-column budget; the app UI displays Rs 1,234.00 per Section 3.1.
